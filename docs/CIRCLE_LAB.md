# Circle Lab results

Generated by `npm run simulate` (1000 circles per cell, seeded, reproducible). The simulator drives the real engine in `src/engine/circle.ts`.

**Model.** Each round every member independently misses their payment with probability *p*. "Full payout" means the recipient received the whole pot (net of the safety-pot share). "Recovery" is total paid out divided by total target over all payouts. Defaults are independent; real groups have correlated defaults (a shared bad month), so treat these as optimistic bounds.
